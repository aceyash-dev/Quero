Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July,2026

Your privacy matters to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information Quero collects, how it is used, how it is protected, and the choices you have regarding your data.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Quero, we may collect:

  • Account information such as your name, email address, and profile details provided during sign in.
  • Messages, prompts, and conversations you choose to save.
  • Files and documents you upload for processing.
  • Basic device information such as browser type, operating system, language, and approximate region.
  • Usage analytics to help improve reliability and performance.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to:

  • Provide AI-powered responses and requested features.
  • Maintain your account and preferences.
  • Improve the quality, performance, and reliability of Quero.
  • Protect against fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access.
  • Comply with legal obligations where required.

3. Uploaded Files

Files uploaded to Quero are processed only to complete the task you request. Temporary uploads are automatically deleted after processing unless explicitly stated otherwise.

4. Conversation History

If conversation history is enabled, your chats may be stored securely so they can be accessed across your devices. You can delete conversations or your entire account at any time.

5. Third-Party Services

Quero relies on trusted third-party providers to operate the service. These may include authentication providers such as Clerk, AI model providers, hosting providers, and infrastructure services.

These providers process only the information necessary to deliver their services and are subject to their own privacy policies.

6. Data Security

We use industry-standard security practices to help protect your information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction. While no online service can guarantee absolute security, protecting your data is a priority.

7. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as necessary to provide the service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our policies.

8. Your Rights

You may have the right to:

  • Access the personal information associated with your account.
  • Correct inaccurate information.
  • Delete your account and associated data.
  • Withdraw consent where applicable.
  • Request additional information about how your data is processed.

9. Children's Privacy

Quero is not intended for children under the minimum age required by applicable law without parental or guardian consent. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically to reflect improvements to Quero or changes in legal requirements. The effective date at the top of this page indicates the latest revision.

11. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please contact us.
