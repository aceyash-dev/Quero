01 / Information

What we collect

Quero is designed to be a place where you can ask questions, explore ideas, and create things. Depending on how you use the service, we may process information needed to provide those experiences.

Information you provide

  • Questions, prompts, messages, and other content you choose to submit.
  • Account information if an account or sign-in feature is used.
  • Information you voluntarily provide when contacting support.

Information collected automatically

We may receive limited technical information such as browser type, device information, approximate location derived from network information, and basic diagnostic data. This information helps us keep Quero functioning, secure, and usable.

Quero does not need every detail about you to answer a question. We aim to collect only what is reasonably necessary for the service.

02 / Purpose

How we use information

Information may be used to operate, maintain, secure, and improve Quero and its related services.

  • Provide and personalize the service.
  • Process and respond to requests.
  • Maintain reliability and performance.
  • Detect abuse, fraud, and security issues.
  • Debug technical problems.
  • Communicate important service information.
  • Improve the overall Quero experience.

We do not treat your personal information as a commodity. The service exists to help you think, not to turn your private questions into an advertising profile.

03 / Disclosure

When information is shared

We may use trusted infrastructure providers and service partners where necessary to operate Quero. Those providers may process information on our behalf and are expected to handle it according to applicable agreements and security requirements.

We may also disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, respond to lawful requests, protect the security of the service, or protect the rights and safety of users and others.

We do not sell personal information as a business model.

04 / Security

Keeping information safe

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

No internet service can promise absolute security. Human beings invented both encryption and phishing, so the universe clearly enjoys irony.

You should avoid submitting passwords, payment credentials, private keys, or other highly sensitive information into ordinary conversations unless the service specifically asks for it and provides an appropriate mechanism.

05 / Control

Your choices

Depending on the features available to you, you may be able to access, correct, delete, or otherwise manage information associated with your use of Quero.

You can also contact us about privacy-related requests. We may need to verify a request before acting on it in order to protect your information.

Some information may need to be retained where required by law, necessary for security, or needed to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.
